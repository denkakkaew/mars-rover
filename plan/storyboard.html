Hardware Specification

A top-down look at the rover, and the components that make it drive, see, and grab.

Fig. 2.1 — Rover platform, top view (forward = up)

Drive platform Mechanics

  • 4× DC geared motors through a dual H-bridge driver (TB6612 / L298N).
  • Differential (skid) steering — forward, backward, left, right.
  • Sized to turn freely inside the 1.4 m box width.

Robotic arm Manipulator

  • 3–4 servo joints + gripper (MG996R / SG90 class).
  • Front-mounted; reaches past the bumper to grip a rock on the surface.
  • Holds the sample secure through transit back to base.

Vision & compute Electronics

  • Front IP camera for driving and close-in alignment.
  • Mast IP camera on a pole with a pan/tilt head (2 servos) to look L/R/up/down.
  • ESP32 main board — Wi-Fi + Bluetooth, runs motor & servo control.
  • LiPo pack + buck regulator for 5V / 3.3V rails.
03

Software & Signal Flow

Control commands and camera video travel on separate paths, so driving stays responsive while two live feeds stream to their own monitors.

Fig. 3.1 — System architecture: control channel (rust) vs. video channel (cyan)

Console app Godot

  • Full-screen on the touchscreen.
  • Drive pad, mast pan/tilt, arm controls, telemetry strip.
  • Commands over Wi-Fi (WebSocket), Bluetooth fallback.

Rover firmware ESP32

  • Parses commands, drives H-bridge + servos.
  • Runs the arm and mast pan/tilt loops.
  • Reports telemetry back to the console.

Video path Wi-Fi

  • Each IP camera streams to its own monitor.
  • Front feed on one screen, mast feed on the other.
  • Video stays off the control channel.
